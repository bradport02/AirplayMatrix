{{ icon('grid') }} Display mode

What the 64x64 panel shows. Currently {% if matrix_mode == 'eq_meter' %}EQ meter{% elif matrix_mode == 'fixed_color' %}fixed colour{% else %}album art{% endif %}. These are mutually exclusive -- the EQ meter takes the whole panel over, so choosing album art or fixed colour also switches it back off.

EQ meter is a live bar-graph equaliser driven by the actual AirPlay audio (a loopback tap on the Pi's own output). It needs a one-time snd-aloop/ALSA setup on the device first -- see docs/install-pi-zero-wh.md's EQ meter section; until that's in place this option does nothing. Of the three modes it is the only one actually wired up today -- it takes effect immediately, while album art and fixed colour are saved but inert (see below).

The album art / fixed colour choice above, and everything below, is not wired to the panel yet. The ESP32 firmware currently only accepts image frames over serial, with no command channel for power, brightness, colour, or a test pattern. Settings saved here are held ready and will take effect once that firmware support ships. (The EQ meter mode is the exception -- it runs on the Pi side, not the firmware's.)
